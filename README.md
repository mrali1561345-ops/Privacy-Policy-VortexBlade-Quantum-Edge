# Privacy Policy for VortexBlade Quantum Edge
Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application VortexBlade: Quantum Edge (herein referred to as "the Application").

# 1. Information Collection and Use
The Application is designed as an arcade game. It does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location).

# 2. Gameplay & Local Storage Data
Game progression statistics, high scores, coins, unlocked ships, and preference settings (audio, difficulty) are stored locally on your device. Clearing app cache or uninstalling the app may reset this local data.

# 3. Third-Party Services
The Application does not currently integrate third-party data tracking, advertising networks, or analytics frameworks that gather user-identifiable information.

# 4. Children's Privacy
The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

# 5. Security
We value your trust in providing us your choice to play the app. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted over networks by the Application, your personal security is preserved locally on your device.

# 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
